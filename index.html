02 / ABOUT

A little about me.

I decided to move into the world of technology because I wanted to find a space where I could make the most of my potential, keep learning, and turn ideas into something real.

What fascinates me most about development is the possibility of starting from an idea and building something from scratch. I especially enjoy the idea of using technology not just to create, but to help people bring their own ideas to life and solve real problems.

I'm currently completing the advanced section of the Python Programming MOOC from the University of Helsinki. At the same time, I'm exploring AI-assisted development and refining my approach to vibe coding, learning how to use AI effectively across different projects, needs and situations.

My goal is to become capable of creating websites, applications, automations and other digital solutions that make everyday life simpler — both for myself and for the people who choose to work with me.

I'm still learning, experimenting and building. This portfolio is part of that journey.
